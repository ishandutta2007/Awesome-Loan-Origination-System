# Awesome-Loan-Origination-System

## Top Loan Origination System (LOS) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Mortgage & Consumer Lending Origination, Underwriting Workflows, Point-of-Sale, Compliance & Digital Lending*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Loan Origination Systems (LOS)**. These systems manage the end-to-end process of taking a loan application from intake through underwriting, approval, closing, and (in some cases) handoff to servicing—covering mortgage, consumer, commercial, and multi-product lending.



**Examples** include Blend, MeridianLink, nCino, TurnKey Lender, Mortgage Cadence, BytePro, Encompass, Finastra Loan IQ, Newgen LOS, and CloudVirga (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured, production-grade LOS platforms (especially U.S. mortgage) are almost entirely commercial due to heavy compliance, investor connectivity, and regulatory requirements. Practical open options exist mainly in the broader lending domain—**Frappe Lending**, Apache Fineract-related work, and experimental LOS projects. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Blend](https://blend.com/)**  

  Digital lending and consumer banking platform widely used for mortgage and consumer loan point-of-sale, application intake, and borrower experience.



- **[MeridianLink](https://www.meridianlink.com/)**  

  Multi-product loan origination platform (including former LendingQB capabilities) serving credit unions, banks, and lenders across consumer, mortgage, and related products.



- **[nCino](https://www.ncino.com/)**  

  Cloud banking platform built on Salesforce that includes commercial, consumer, and mortgage loan origination, credit analysis, and related banking workflows.



- **[TurnKey Lender](https://www.turnkey-lender.com/)**  

  Configurable loan origination and management software aimed at digital lenders and alternative finance providers.



- **[Mortgage Cadence](https://www.mortgagecadence.com/)**  

  Mortgage-focused loan origination and secondary marketing platform used by lenders for end-to-end mortgage processing.



- **[BytePro](https://www.bytepro.com/)**  

  Mortgage loan origination software popular with brokers and smaller lenders for application
